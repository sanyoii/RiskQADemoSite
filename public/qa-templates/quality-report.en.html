Synthetic Order Book teaching scenario, not a real exchange test. Based on this site's stale-sequence Fail Demo. Prices, quantities and times are supplemental teaching assumptions. Runs, defects and evidence names are synthetic; there was no real product execution, attached evidence or customer approval.

What this document answers

Separate known issues, execution completeness, exit decision and untested scope. Results reference the Run Log; derive the overall conclusion from evidence.

Blank template

[Required] applies to every case. [Conditional] is required when applicable; explain when it is not. Copy the text into your case documents and replace the brackets. This static page has no editing or saving function.

Filled example: all cases completed, exit not met

DEMO-QUALITY-MDQ · v1.2

Scope: three synthetic Order Book cases in DEMO-SPEC-MDQ v1.2. Illustrative cutoff 2026-10-26 17:30 UTC+08:00; product build-demo-1, contract DEMO-DC-MDQ v1.0.

DimensionThis batch
Known issuesDEF-MDQ-004: stale-sequence overwrite, S1 · Triaged · demo, not fixed; no approved risk acceptance.
Execution completenessSelected 3 cases: 2 Pass, 1 Fail. Completion (2+1)/3 = 3/3 = 100%. Not Run / Blocked / Skipped are all 0 (synthetic). Completion is not pass rate. If zero cases are selected, display N/A.
Exit decisionCondition 1: all required steps and checkpoints completed in the synthetic scenario; no real current evidence supplied. Condition 2: unmet, an unresolved S1 remains. Condition 3: synthetic traceability is complete without unexplained gaps. The batch does not meet exit criteria.
Untested scopeEqual sequences, gaps, reorder buffering, zero quantities, missing values, load, real feeds and orders are untested. The product / integration owner must confirm specifications before risk-based planning. These exclusions are not included in the three-case denominator.
Review / limitsAll results are teaching entries. No real product execution, human acceptance or release approval occurred. Document v1.2 did not change this site's historical Runs.
Derived summary (example only): Blocked

Despite 100% completion, 1 unresolved S1 remains. Plan a fix and new Runs before reassessment. There is no independent conclusion field; this is not the current status of a real product.

Ready means agreed test-delivery conditions are met. Go still requires current evidence and an authorized decision. A fix claim or a rerun that has not happened cannot authorize release.
