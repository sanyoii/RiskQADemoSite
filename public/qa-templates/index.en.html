Apply this to your engagement

  1. Copy the blank specification and enter actual sources and versions.
  2. Rederive data, units, timing, expected values, stop conditions and evidence for the target product. Retain document responsibilities and traceability; changing only the product name is insufficient.
  3. Use the Case template to define observable checkpoints. Obtain required approval before operating the product.
  4. Record each execution in a new Run, preserving history. Use the Quality Report to derive the overall status.
