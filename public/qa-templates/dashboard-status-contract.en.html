What this document answers

08 Dashboard Status Contract defines how an approved 07 Release Quality Summary becomes a public projection. Runs retain execution facts, 07 retains judgment, and 08 defines publishable fields and validation.

Separate product status from data status

StatusMeaning
BlockedA trustworthy, same-version unresolved S1, unaccepted S2 or critical-path nonconformance. Retain known issues when evidence expires and list missing data separately.
At RiskNo blocking issue, but remaining defects or accepted risk exist. Record accountability and follow-up; this does not authorize release.
UnknownNo trustworthy known blocking or residual issue, but criteria, evidence or review are incomplete. Corrupt or subject-mismatched data cannot establish product facts.
ReadyAll four dimensions support test delivery, no known residual defect / accepted risk remains, and a human-owned assessment exists. Publication still needs Go. Historical Ready is not current approval.
Data StatusFresh / Stale / Invalid / Not Configured / Unreachable describe source availability. They do not directly establish product quality or provide live monitoring.

Correction and recovery

On source revision, identify affected cases and append required Runs and reviews. Invalid data keeps the last-known-good snapshot plus a failure marker; do not rewrite old snapshots. Corrections, withdrawals, rollback and post-publication checks follow existing ownership. Sending, publishing or deploying still requires authority. See source revisions, review disagreements and AI misjudgments.
